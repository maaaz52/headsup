Privacy

Headsup is designed to work locally on your computer.

Calendar links

Calendar subscription links and preferences are stored on your device. Treat private calendar links like passwords because anyone with the link may be able to read that calendar.

Network access

Headsup connects directly to the calendar subscription URLs you add so it can retrieve upcoming events. It does not require a Headsup account.

Analytics

The desktop application does not include advertising or analytics services.

Removing data

Remove a calendar inside the app to stop using its subscription link. Uninstalling Headsup and deleting its application data removes locally saved settings.
